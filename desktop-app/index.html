Privacy and terms

In plain language

What we keep on our servers

Your email address and name. For each rewrite: when it happened, which format, which app, and how many words. Your daily totals. If you add your own AI key, that key, encrypted. We do not keep the text you write or the rewrite you get back.

What stays on your computer

Your History, with the full text of what you wrote and what PolishAI wrote. It is encrypted, and you can delete any entry, or all of it, from the History screen.

Who sees your text

When you rewrite something, the text is sent through PolishAI's server to an AI provider, and the answer comes back. Your free daily rewrites are processed by Google Gemini. Google's terms say that content sent on its free tier can be used to improve its products and may be read by people. If that matters, add your own key from Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic under Settings, so your own account's terms apply. Do not rewrite passwords, financial details or other sensitive information.

Deleting your data

Settings has a Delete my account button. It removes your account, everything we keep about you on our servers, and the History on this computer. It cannot be undone.

Using PolishAI

PolishAI is provided as it is, with no promise that it will always work or stay free. Free rewrites have a daily limit for each person. You are responsible for what you write and send. Please do not misuse the service or try to overload it. We may change or stop it, or close accounts that abuse it.
